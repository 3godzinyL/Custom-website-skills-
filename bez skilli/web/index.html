How it works

Three steps. Then it keeps up with you.

  1. 1

    Tell it what's on your plate

    Type naturally — “finish the proposal by Friday, call Marta, prep slides”. Review the proposed tasks and apply them.

  2. 2

    Get a realistic day

    Kedgely ranks everything and places it into your working hours with buffers, around fixed meetings you lock in place.

  3. 3

    Re-plan when reality hits

    Running late? One request rebuilds the rest of the day, raises what slipped and moves overflow to tomorrow.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Write to hello@kedgely.app.

What makes Kedgely different from a to-do app or calendar?

To-do apps store tasks and calendars store events; neither decides when work should happen or fixes the plan when it breaks. Kedgely schedules tasks into your real working hours and re-plans the rest of the day when something overruns.

Does the assistant change my data on its own?

No. The assistant prepares a proposal — tasks to add, changes, a new schedule — and you apply all of it, part of it or none. It can only touch your own tasks, projects and schedule.

Which AI model do you use?

The app connects to a large language model through a server-side integration (Anthropic's Claude by default). Your instance shows whether answers come from the live model or from the built-in rule-based demo engine, which is used when no model is configured. Demo answers are always labelled.

How do I get access during the beta?

Join the waitlist below. We send personal invite codes in batches. If you already have a code, create an account and enter it at sign-up.

What happens to my data?

We store only what the product needs: your account, tasks, projects, schedule and assistant history. You can export or delete it all from Settings. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I use it with a team?

Today Kedgely plans one person's day. The Team plan raises limits for small teams; shared projects are on the roadmap, not available yet.

Will the beta stay free?

Access during the beta is free. Before any paid plan starts we will tell you in advance, and nothing is charged automatically.
