3. The AI assistant

When the live AI mode is enabled, the text you send to the assistant together with a minimal context (titles, dates, estimates and ids of your own open tasks, projects and today's schedule) is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic, PBC) to generate a response. Data of other users is never included. Without a configured provider, a rule-based engine runs entirely on our server and nothing leaves it. The provider acts as our processor; transfers outside the EEA rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Cookies

Kedgely uses only strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie after you sign in (and a separate one for administrators). They are HttpOnly, contain a random identifier and are not used for tracking. No consent banner is required for these cookies.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, to restrict or object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Most of this is self-service: Settings → Data & privacy lets you download all your data as JSON and permanently delete your account. For anything else, write to privacy@kedgely.app. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes UODO).

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; session tokens are stored only as hashes; all access to your data is checked on the server and scoped to your account. No system is perfectly secure — if we become aware of a breach affecting you, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or by e-mail before they take effect.
