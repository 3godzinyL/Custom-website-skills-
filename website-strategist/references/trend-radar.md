# Category radar: from an idea to what wins in that category right now

The radar turns a one-line idea ("strona dla mojej aplikacji do planowania dnia",
"strona firmy myjącej kostkę") into evidence: what this category is, who leads it,
what is moving in it this season, what every serious site in it shows, and where the
gaps are. Everything the strategist proposes later must trace back to this file's
output or to the user's own data.

## 1. Decode the idea (5 minutes, before any search)

Write, from the prompt, the brief or the example site:

- **Teza strony** (one sentence): „<Produkt/firma> pomaga <komu> <osiągnąć co> bez
  <bólu>, inaczej niż <alternatywa, z której korzystają dziś>.”
- **Category and sub-category**, in the market's words *and* in global English terms
  (they lead to different sources): „planer dnia z AI” = *AI daily planner, AI calendar,
  auto-scheduling app*; „mycie kostki brukowej” = *pressure washing, paver cleaning*.
- **Buyer and user** (often different: parent/child, company/employee), **market**
  (city, country, EU, global), **price level**, **business model**, **main conversion**.
- **Category type**, because it changes what the site must do first:

| Type | Visitor's main question | The site must lead with |
|---|---|---|
| New category (people don't search for it yet) | "What is this and why do I need it?" | the problem and a demonstration |
| Crowded category | "Why this one and not X?" | the difference, proof, comparison |
| Local service | "Can they do it here, well, for how much, when?" | area, price, proof, fast contact |
| Commodity shop | "Is it cheaper/faster/safer here?" | price, delivery, returns, reviews |
| High-trust (health, money, law, children) | "Can I trust them?" | expertise, credentials, transparency |

If the idea is too vague to name the category, ask at most 3 questions in one message;
otherwise take the most likely reading, state it, and continue.

## 2. Keyword map (5–10 minutes)

Build four rings, in the audience's language and in English:

1. **Category**: what people call the thing („planer AI”, *AI planner app*).
2. **Problem**: how people describe the pain before they know the solution („jak
   ogarnąć dzień”, *procrastination*, *ADHD planner*, „kostka zielona od mchu”).
3. **Solution / feature**: *voice to tasks*, *auto reschedule*, „mycie ciśnieniowe
   z impregnacją”.
4. **Comparison**: *best X 2026*, *X alternative*, *X vs Y*, „ranking”, „opinie”,
   „cennik”, „ile kosztuje”, plus the city for local services.

Expand with: Google autocomplete and "People also ask", related searches, Google Trends
(12 months, the right country: rising queries and direction), forum and Reddit thread
titles, YouTube titles in the niche, App Store / Google Play category names, G2 /
Capterra category names, Allegro / Ceneo categories for products. With a keyword MCP
(DataForSEO, Ahrefs, Semrush) add volume and difficulty; without one, give direction
only (↑ ↓ →) and say so.

Output table: keyword → intent (informational / comparison / buy / local) → where it
lives on the site (hero, section, FAQ, guide, landing page) → direction.

## 3. Find the leaders (10–20 minutes)

Run the queries in both languages, restricted to the last 12 months where the tool
allows:
- `best <category> 2026`, `top <category> apps`, `<category> alternatives`,
  `<leader> vs`, „najlepszy <kategoria>”, „ranking <kategoria> 2026”,
  „<usługa> <miasto>”, „<usługa> <miasto> cennik”.
- **Market leaders**: G2 / Capterra grid leaders, App Store and Google Play top charts in
  the category, Trustpilot top rated, Allegro / Ceneo bestsellers, Google Maps best
  rated in the city (local), Similarweb rank if available.
- **Fast risers**: Product Hunt top launches of the month/year in the category, funding
  news ("raises", „pozyskał”), newly featured apps, accounts growing on TikTok/YouTube.
- **Design benchmarks in the category**: Awwwards, Godly, Land-book, Lapa Ninja,
  SaaS Landing Page, One Page Love, Mobbin (app flows).

Choose 5–10 sites: 3–5 leaders, 2–3 risers, 1–2 design benchmarks; for a local service
3–5 strongest local competitors plus 1–2 national leaders. Say why each is on the list.

## 4. The news pulse: what is moving now (10–15 minutes)

Look back 3–6 months (12 in slow niches) for signals that change what visitors expect:
- Google News for category keywords; industry media; Hacker News and Reddit for tech
  products; Product Hunt launches; YouTube/TikTok topics in the niche;
- "What's new" pages, changelogs and pricing changes of the leaders;
- regulation and platform changes that touch the category (EU AI Act transparency,
  European Accessibility Act, Omnibus price rules, GPSR, KSeF e-invoices, consumer law,
  Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, app store rules, BLIK and payment trends);
- incidents (data breaches, AI failures, scandals) that create a new fear to answer.

Record each signal: **what, source, date, who it affects, how the site can use it**:
- **Ride** — a feature, section or content piece that meets the wave,
- **Answer** — an objection the wave created ("czy moje nagrania trafiają do AI?"),
- **Avoid** — fading, legally risky or audience-irrelevant.

Status: **Sprawdzone** (proven, converts) · **Rosnące** (emerging, worth testing) ·
**Moda** (hype, easily misused) · **Gaśnie** (fading). A trend needs two independent
sources inside the window, or one primary source (official release, regulation text,
the leader's own page).

## 5. Teardown of leader sites (20–40 minutes)

Visit the real pages of each leader: home, pricing, product/service, about, one guide
or blog post, the start of signup/checkout. With a browser tool, look at desktop and
mobile and take screenshots; without one, fetch the pages and say that layout was not
seen. One card per site:

```markdown
### <Nazwa> — <URL> · obejrzane <RRRR-MM-DD> · <lider / wschodzący / design / lokalny>
- Obietnica w hero (dosłownie + tłumaczenie): …
- Dowód tuż pod hero: …
- Kolejność sekcji na głównej: 1 … 2 … 3 …
- Oferta i ceny: plany, trial/freemium, kotwice cenowe, gwarancja
- Dowody: liczba opinii, logotypy, case studies, liczby, certyfikaty, media
- CTA główne / dodatkowe; tarcie (pola formularza, karta przy trialu?)
- Interakcje: kalkulator, demo, quiz, konfigurator, przed/po, mapa
- Treści: poradniki, porównania, szablony, narzędzia za darmo
- Zaufanie i prawo: prywatność, informacja o AI, gdzie dane, gwarancje
- Język designu (1 zdanie): …
- Najlepsze, co robią: … · Słabe miejsce: …
```

Then the **feature matrix**: rows = sections, features, content types, proof types;
columns = the sites; cells ✓ / ✗ / częściowo; last column = share of leaders that have
it. Read 1–3 star reviews of the leaders (App Store, G2, Google Maps, Trustpilot) —
recurring complaints are the cheapest source of differentiators.

## 6. Synthesis: standard, differentiators, gaps

- **Standard kategorii** — present at ≥ 60–70 % of the leaders, or expected by every
  visitor of this category. Missing it costs trust. Only category-specific items:
  universal basics (legal pages, contact, SEO basics, speed, accessibility, security)
  are the website-maker's foundation and are *not* repeated here.
- **Wyróżniki** — what the best 1–3 do and others don't.
- **Luki** — what nobody does well: complaints in reviews, unanswered questions in
  forums, conventions that frustrate users. The biggest opportunities live here.
- **Pytania odbiorców** — from "People also ask", forums and reviews → FAQ, guides,
  comparison pages, answers that AI search engines can quote.
- **Konwencje kategorii** — what visitors expect to see (section order, words, how
  prices are shown) and where breaking a convention would stand out without confusing.

## 7. Evidence rules and time budget

- Every claim has a source and a date; the list goes to SITE-BRIEF §16 (Źródła).
- Never invent numbers (traffic, conversion lift, market size, review counts). An
  estimate is labelled „szacunek” with the method.
- Quote competitors' copy only to analyse it; the site's copy must be original.
- Without web access: say so, use `niche-benchmarks.md` and `trends-2026.md` as the
  baseline, mark findings „do weryfikacji”, and suggest a research MCP once.
- **Light radar** (Mode B default): 45–90 minutes of tool work, 5–8 sites, 10–20
  sources. **Deep radar** (user asks, large or competitive project, Mode A for a big
  site): 2–3 hours, 10–15 sites, 30+ sources, screenshots of each leader.
- Research MCPs (Firecrawl, Exa, Playwright, DataForSEO) make the deep radar cheaper in
  tokens — suggest them once, never install them yourself (`mcp-toolkit.md`).
