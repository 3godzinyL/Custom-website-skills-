## 1. Inventory checklist

Record what exists (and where), page by page:
- **Pages and structure**: sitemap, navigation, footer, depth, orphan pages, 404.
- **Above the fold** on desktop and mobile: headline, subline, CTA, visual, trust snippet.
- **Offer**: services/products, prices or ranges, packages, comparison, guarantees.
- **Trust**: reviews (source, count, recency), case studies, portfolio, team, company
  data, certificates (real), media mentions, years in business, guarantees, policies.
- **Conversion path**: primary/secondary CTAs, forms (fields, friction), booking,
  checkout steps, contact options (phone, chat, WhatsApp), response time promise.
- **Content**: blog/guides, FAQ, landing pages per service/location, video, downloads.
- **SEO basics**: titles, descriptions, H1s, internal linking, schema.org, local SEO
  (Google Business Profile, NAP consistency), indexable pages, sitemap, speed.
- **UX/mobile**: navigation, tap targets, readability, sticky actions, filters/search.
- **Design coherence**: tokens consistency, typography, imagery quality, AI-slop tells.
- **Retention**: newsletter, accounts, loyalty, reminders, remarketing (consent-based),
  community, content cadence.
- **Monetization/AOV**: upsells, bundles, subscriptions, free-shipping threshold,
  vouchers, B2B offers, financing.
- **Integrations/automation**: CRM, mailing, booking tools, marketplaces, feeds,
  invoicing, review requests.
- **Measurement**: analytics, conversion events, Search Console, call tracking.
- **Legal basics**: privacy policy, cookies consent, terms for shops, Omnibus, etc.
  (presence check only, not legal advice).

## 2. Scoring rubric

Score each area 1–5 and justify in one line. 1 = missing/broken, 3 = typical for the
niche, 5 = leader level.

| Area | 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Offer clarity | visitor knows in 5 s what, for whom, how much, what next |
| Trust and proof | recent real reviews with source, concrete cases, faces, guarantees |
| Conversion path | one clear action per page, low-friction forms, contact from any screen |
| Content and SEO | pages for every service/location/category intent, helpful guides, schema |
| UX and mobile | thumb-friendly, fast, sticky action, no dead ends |
| Design coherence | specific concept, consistent system, no AI-slop tells |
| Retention | reasons and channels to come back (newsletter, account, reminders, loyalty) |
| Monetization / AOV | bundles, upsells, subscriptions, thresholds suited to the niche |
| Integrations / automation | data flows without manual work (CRM, reviews, feeds, invoices) |
| Measurement | conversions tracked, owner can see what works |

Show the scores as a table with the niche-typical level next to them, so the owner sees
where they are below the market.

## 3. Evidence sources

Every recommendation needs at least one:
- **Competitor/leader evidence**: "Firma X i Y mają kalkulator; Z ma rezerwację online".
- **Research**: Baymard (e-commerce UX), Nielsen Norman Group, Google (Core Web Vitals,
  Search Central), platform data from reputable sources.
- **Principle**: established UX/conversion principle explained in one sentence.
- **Site data** if available: analytics, Search Console queries, heatmaps.

Avoid unverifiable statistics from trend blogs. If you cite a number, say where it is
from; if you can't, don't use a number.

## 4. ICE prioritization

For each recommendation:
- **Impact** (1–10): expected effect on the main goal (leads, sales, bookings).
- **Confidence** (1–10): how strong the evidence is.
- **Ease** (1–10): 10 = an hour, 1 = months.
- **ICE = (I × C × E) / 100**, sort descending.

Then group into the roadmap:
- **Teraz (1–7 dni)**: inseparable requirements missing + top quick wins.
- **Następnie (2–6 tygodni)**: important features and content.
- **Później (kwartał+)**: larger differentiators, platform changes.
