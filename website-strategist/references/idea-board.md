# Idea board and directions: what the user decides

The radar produces evidence; the idea board turns it into decisions. The user accepts or
rejects ideas and picks a direction, and only then is the SITE-BRIEF written. The maker
builds what was accepted and never re-proposes what was rejected.

## 1. Generate ideas through ten lenses

For each lens ask: *what would make a visitor from this category think "this one gets
it"?* Use the radar's standard, differentiators, gaps and pulse as raw material.

| Lens | Typical ideas |
|---|---|
| 1. Proof | interactive demo in the browser, before/after, live counters of real data, case study with numbers, video of the real work |
| 2. Try before committing | calculator, configurator, quote in 30 seconds, free tool, sample, sandbox |
| 3. Price clarity | visible prices or ranges, "ile kosztuje" explainer, comparison with alternatives, no-surprise guarantee |
| 4. Speed and convenience | one-field signup, booking in two taps, BLIK / Apple Pay, WhatsApp or callback, delivery date shown |
| 5. Trust and risk reversal | real team and address, guarantees, transparent data and AI use, certificates, response-time promise |
| 6. Content and search | answers to top audience questions, comparison pages, guides, facts AI answers can quote (GEO), local pages with unique content |
| 7. Community and social proof | waitlist with referrals, reviews with photos, user stories, partner logos (real) |
| 8. Personalisation | quiz or finder, "dla kogo" segments, language/region, saved preferences |
| 9. Retention | accounts that give value, reminders, subscriptions, loyalty, follow-up e-mails |
| 10. Signature moment | one memorable interaction tied to the concept (website-design turns it into motion and layout) |

Filter every idea through: evidence (radar or the user's data), fit to this audience,
effort, legal and dark-pattern risk (fake urgency, fake reviews and pre-ticked consents
are illegal in the EU), and impact on the main conversion.

## 2. Idea card

| # | Pomysł | Skąd (dowód + data) | Dla odbiorcy, bo… | Typ | Opcja makera | Nakład | Wpływ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Typ**: **Standard** (category must-have) · **Fala** (rides a current signal) ·
  **Wyróżnik** (beats the leaders) · **Odważne** (bold bet, higher risk or effort).
- **Opcja makera**: the smallest website-maker option that can deliver it (1 front,
  2 backend, 3 panel + payments, 4 ultimate).
- **Nakład**: S (hours) / M (1–3 days) / L (a week+). **Wpływ**: niski / średni / wysoki,
  with a one-line reason.
- Mark 3–5 cards **★ rekomendowane**: the best value for this business now.

## 3. Directions (2–3)

A direction is a positioning choice: it changes what the site puts first, how it talks
and what it sells hardest. Every direction must be buildable with the same product.

```markdown
### Kierunek A — <nazwa> („<jedno zdanie obietnicy>”)
- Wygrywa u: … · Traci u: …
- Co się zmienia na stronie: obietnica w hero, 3 pierwsze sekcje, oferta/ceny, ton, CTA
- Potrzebne pomysły: #…
- Przykład z radaru: <kto robi podobnie i gdzie>
- Ryzyko: …
```

Typical directions to adapt: premium expert · najszybszy i najprostszy · najtańszy
i przejrzysty · specjalista od jednej niszy · lokalny bohater · społeczność ·
narzędzie najpierw (darmowe narzędzie jako wejście) · autorytet treści.

When the radar shows a product opportunity (a neglected segment, a pricing model the
leaders don't offer, a B2B version, a bundle), add up to three **Kierunki rozwoju
produktu**, clearly labelled as product ideas, not site features.

## 4. The acceptance round — stop and wait

Show the radar summary, the board and the directions in one message, then stop:

```markdown
## Radar kategorii — w skrócie
<5–8 punktów: teza, typ kategorii, liderzy, najważniejsze sygnały, największa luka>

## W pakiecie (standard kategorii — dodam, chyba że coś wykreślisz)
- …

## Pomysły do decyzji
| # | Pomysł | Dlaczego | Typ | Opcja | Nakład | ★ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Kierunki
**A** … · **B** … · **C** …

## Jeszcze kilka pytań
<najwyżej 5 pytań z discovery-interview, na które radar nie odpowiedział>

Odpisz np.: „TAK 1, 2, 5; NIE 3; MOŻE 6; kierunek B” albo „bierz rekomendowane”.
```

Rules:
- At most ~15 ideas to decide; standard items go in the package by default, so the user
  spends attention on real choices.
- If the user answers only partly, take the recommended defaults for the rest and say so.
- Confirm the decisions in 3 lines before writing.

## 5. Recording decisions (SITE-BRIEF §6)

- **Zaakceptowane** → become requirements with their maker option and acceptance
  criteria.
- **Później** (MOŻE) → listed as upgrades the maker may offer at the end of the build,
  not as new proposals.
- **Odrzucone — nie proponować ponownie** → kept with the reason if the user gave one.
- **Kierunek** → drives the hero promise, section order and tone; website-design gets
  it as the mood for the demos.
