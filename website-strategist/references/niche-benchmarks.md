# Niche benchmarks

Per niche: **Inseparable** (without it the site loses customers), **Differentiators**
(what the best do), **Trends worth it in 2026** (with a reason), **Growth levers**,
**KPIs**, **Typical gaps** (what audits usually find). Built-in knowledge — confirm with
live research when possible. Combine lists for hybrid businesses.

## 1. Local services (cleaning, paving, renovation, plumbing, gardening, removals)
- **Inseparable:** service area, price ranges, real before/after, reviews from Google,
  click-to-call on mobile, quote form, company data, response time.
- **Differentiators:** instant price calculator, online date booking, photo-based
  quote, per-town landing pages, guarantee in writing, process video.
- **Trends worth it:** WhatsApp/SMS contact (customers prefer messaging), maintenance
  subscriptions (recurring revenue), automated review request after job (review count is
  the #1 local trust signal), AI-assisted quote from photos (speed wins the lead).
- **Growth levers:** local SEO (Google Business Profile posts/photos, town pages),
  seasonal offers, referral discounts, B2B (housing communities, property managers).
- **KPIs:** leads/week, lead → job rate, cost per lead, reviews/month, response time.
- **Typical gaps:** no prices at all, stock photos instead of real jobs, phone not
  clickable, no area info, one page for all services (weak SEO), no reviews visible.

## 2. Appointments (hair, barber, beauty, physio, dental, clinic, therapy)
- **Inseparable:** online booking with real availability, price list with durations,
  team with specialisations, location/parking, hours, cancellation rules, reviews.
- **Differentiators:** choose-your-specialist, deposits against no-shows, waitlist,
  gift vouchers online, treatment pages with before/after (consent).
- **Trends worth it:** reminders and rebooking prompts (reduce no-shows), memberships/
  packages, loyalty, content answering "ile kosztuje / czy boli / jak się przygotować".
- **Growth levers:** retention (rebook rate), vouchers before holidays, local SEO per
  treatment, Instagram-to-booking path.
- **KPIs:** bookings/week, no-show rate, rebook rate, voucher sales.
- **Typical gaps:** booking hidden behind external link without context, PDF price list,
  no team info, no cancellation policy.

## 3. Food and hospitality
- **Inseparable:** HTML menu with prices and allergens, hours (incl. holidays), map,
  reservation/order, phone, real photos.
- **Differentiators:** own online ordering (no aggregator commission), daily specials
  from backend, pre-orders (cakes, holidays), catering configurator, private events page.
- **Trends worth it:** pickup time slots, QR ordering at table, gift cards, SMS/
  newsletter for specials, loyalty stamps digital.
- **Growth levers:** holiday pre-order campaigns, B2B catering/lunch subscriptions,
  events, Google Maps photos and reviews.
- **KPIs:** reservations, online orders, average order value, repeat customers.
- **Typical gaps:** menu only as PDF or Instagram image, outdated hours, no reservation,
  slow heavy images.

## 4. E-commerce (general)
- **Inseparable:** search + filters, rich product pages (photos, variants, specs,
  availability, delivery time and cost), reviews, Polish payment methods (BLIK first),
  InPost + courier, guest checkout, returns info, regulamin, Omnibus lowest price,
  GPSR data, order e-mails, fast mobile.
- **Differentiators:** free-shipping progress bar, bundles, "frequently bought
  together", back-in-stock alerts, reviews with photos, wishlist, express checkout,
  buy now pay later.
- **Trends worth it:** guided selling (quizzes, AI shopping assistant) for products
  with many options; short product videos; UGC; subscriptions for consumables;
  post-purchase upsell; product comparison for high-consideration goods.
- **Growth levers:** abandoned cart flow, email/SMS lifecycle (welcome, replenish,
  win-back), Google Merchant/Allegro channels, loyalty/referral, AOV via thresholds and
  bundles, content SEO (guides, comparisons).
- **KPIs:** conversion rate, AOV, cart abandonment, repeat purchase rate, CAC, LTV.
- **Typical gaps:** delivery cost revealed late, no BLIK, weak product photos, missing
  size/spec info, forced account, no abandoned cart e-mails, slow mobile.

Sub-niche notes:
- **Fashion:** size charts + fit info, on-model photos, free/easy returns, lookbooks,
  drops with notify-me.
- **Cosmetics/food/supplements:** ingredients, usage, certificates (real), subscriptions,
  regimen builders, sampling.
- **Electronics/furniture:** specs and comparison, 3D/AR, configurators, financing,
  delivery + installation services, showroom booking.
- **Handmade/art:** maker story, process, custom orders, limited drops, gift options.

## 5. Accommodation and tourism
- **Inseparable:** direct booking engine with live prices, room pages with photos and
  amenities, policies, location/attractions, reviews, multi-language.
- **Differentiators:** best-price guarantee for direct booking, packages, upsells at
  booking, virtual tours, local guides.
- **Trends worth it:** direct-booking perks (reduce OTA commissions), flexible
  cancellation options, vouchers, digital check-in.
- **KPIs:** direct booking share, occupancy, ADR, upsell revenue.
- **Typical gaps:** booking only via Booking.com link, no prices, outdated galleries.

## 6. SaaS and apps
- **Inseparable:** clear value proposition, real product visuals, pricing page, social
  proof (real), docs/help, security/privacy info, sign-up flow, changelog.
- **Differentiators:** interactive demo/tour, ROI calculator, templates gallery,
  integrations directory, comparison pages, free tools.
- **Trends worth it:** product-led onboarding, usage-based pricing explainer, AI
  features with clear limits, community/templates.
- **KPIs:** visitor → sign-up, activation, trial → paid, churn.
- **Typical gaps:** abstract hero ("platform for growth"), no pricing, fake-looking
  screenshots, no comparison pages.

## 7. Agencies, freelancers, creatives
- **Inseparable:** selected work with real case studies and results, services, process,
  who you are, contact with brief form.
- **Differentiators:** filterable portfolio, starting prices/packages, booking calls,
  insights content, availability indicator.
- **Trends worth it:** case studies with measurable outcomes, short video walkthroughs,
  productized services with fixed prices.
- **KPIs:** qualified inquiries, inquiry → project rate, average project value.
- **Typical gaps:** portfolio without context, no prices hint, generic "we are
  passionate" copy, no process.

## 8. Personal brands, coaches, courses
- **Inseparable:** credibility, clear offers with prices, real testimonials, lead
  magnet + newsletter, checkout/booking.
- **Differentiators:** webinars, community, payment plans, course previews, quizzes.
- **Trends worth it:** cohort programmes, memberships, short-form video integration,
  email-first launches.
- **KPIs:** list growth, lead → buyer, refund rate, LTV.

## 9. Real estate
- **Inseparable:** listings with filters, floor plans, availability table, prices (check
  current Polish rules on price transparency for developers), location, contact/viewing.
- **Differentiators:** interactive building/floor selector, 3D tours, mortgage
  calculator, price history, construction progress.
- **KPIs:** viewing requests, lead quality, time to sell.

## 10. Fitness and sports
- **Inseparable:** schedule from backend, memberships and prices, trainers, facilities,
  sign-up/trial, accepted sport cards info.
- **Differentiators:** class booking with limits and waitlist, member portal, challenges.
- **KPIs:** trials, trial → member, retention, class fill rate.

## 11. Automotive
- **Inseparable:** services with prices from, booking, brands served, location, reviews;
  dealers: inventory with filters and full vehicle data, financing.
- **Differentiators:** repair status updates, service reminders, detailing packages
  configurator, trade-in valuation.

## 12. B2B and industrial
- **Inseparable:** capabilities/products with specs, industries, certificates (real),
  datasheets, references, RFQ form with file upload, contacts per department.
- **Differentiators:** B2B portal with client prices, configurators, CAD downloads,
  distributor locator, multi-language.
- **KPIs:** RFQs, RFQ → order, distributor leads.
- **Typical gaps:** "about us" heavy, products buried, no downloadable specs, Polish only
  for export markets.

## 13. Education
- **Inseparable:** programme, staff, fees, enrolment process and dates, documents,
  location, parent info.
- **Differentiators:** online enrolment, parent portal, events calendar, trial lessons.

## 14. NGO
- **Inseparable:** mission, concrete results, transparency (KRS, reports), donate with
  PL payment methods, 1,5% info, volunteering.
- **Differentiators:** recurring donations, impact calculator, campaign pages, stories.

## 15. Media and blogs
- **Inseparable:** fast readable article template, categories, search, author pages,
  dates, related content, newsletter.
- **Differentiators:** topic hubs, memberships/paywall, podcasts, table of contents,
  AI search over archive.
- **KPIs:** returning readers, newsletter sign-ups, pages/session, revenue/1000 visits.
