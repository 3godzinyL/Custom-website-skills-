# Trends 2026: what is worth it

Built-in knowledge as of late 2026. Trends move fast, so confirm with live research
when tools allow and cite what you saw. Status:
**Sprawdzone** (proven, converts) · **Rosnące** (emerging, worth testing) ·
**Ryzyko mody** (fad or easily misused). Recommend a trend only with a reason tied to
the audience.

## 1. Search and discovery
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visibility in AI answers (Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Perplexity), also called GEO | Rosnące, ważne | clear factual pages (prices, areas, hours, specs), FAQ with direct answers, schema.org, author/expert pages, consistent business data across the web, comparison and "ile kosztuje" pages; measure referrals from AI tools |
| Local pack + Google Business Profile | Sprawdzone | review count, recency and replies; photos; posts; categories; NAP consistency |
| Topic hubs and helpful guides | Sprawdzone | one strong page per intent beats many thin pages |
| Programmatic town/service pages | Ryzyko mody | only with unique content per town (real jobs, photos), otherwise thin content |
| `llms.txt` | Ryzyko mody / eksperyment | cheap to add, unclear adoption by major engines; never a priority |
| Video in search (YouTube, short clips on pages) | Rosnące | how-to and before/after clips for services, product demos |

## 2. Conversion and UX
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile-first with sticky action bar | Sprawdzone | call / book / quote reachable with one thumb |
| Transparent pricing and instant calculators | Sprawdzone | local services, B2B quotes, subscriptions |
| Express checkout: BLIK, Apple Pay, Google Pay | Sprawdzone (PL) | BLIK first in Poland |
| Buy now, pay later (PayPo, Klarna, Twisto-type) | Sprawdzone for AOV > ~200 zł | show in cart and product page |
| Online booking with real availability | Sprawdzone | services, beauty, health, fitness |
| Messaging contact (WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS) | Sprawdzone | younger audiences and local services |
| Guided selling (quizzes, finders) | Sprawdzone for complex catalogues | cosmetics, coffee, bikes, mattresses |
| AI shopping/service assistant | Rosnące | useful with large catalogue or many FAQs; must answer from real data, hand off to a human, disclose it is AI |
| Reviews with photos/video, UGC | Sprawdzone | verified-purchase marking (Omnibus) |
| "Someone just bought…" popups, fake countdowns | Ryzyko mody / zabronione gdy fałszywe | dark patterns; fake urgency or reviews are illegal in the EU |
| Passkeys / passwordless login | Rosnące | faster, safer login for accounts |

## 3. Commerce
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Subscriptions / replenishment | Sprawdzone for consumables | coffee, cosmetics, pet food, supplements |
| Bundles, free-shipping threshold bar | Sprawdzone | raise AOV |

## 4. Design
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Typography-led, editorial layouts | Sprawdzone | expressive heading faces, variable fonts |
| Real photography and authentic content | Sprawdzone | beats stock and AI imagery for trust |
| Purposeful micro-interactions | Sprawdzone | feedback, state changes, before/after |
| Native scroll-driven animations, View Transitions | Rosnące | progressive enhancement, reduced motion |
| Bento grids | Ryzyko mody | overused in AI-generated sites; use only when content fits |
| Glassmorphism, purple gradients, glowing blobs | Ryzyko mody | AI-slop tells |
| Neubrutalism | Niszowe | youth/creative brands only, with a strong system |
| 3D/WebGL heroes | Niszowe | premium tech and portfolios; performance cost |
| AI-generated imagery | Ryzyko | never for real products, results, people or reviews; trust and legal risk |
| Dark mode | Opcjonalne | design it properly or skip |

## 5. Technology and performance
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Core Web Vitals with INP | Sprawdzone | INP < 200 ms; heavy JS hurts |
| Islands / partial hydration (Astro), server rendering | Sprawdzone | fast content sites |
| AVIF/WebP, responsive images, font subsetting | Sprawdzone | biggest quick win |
| PWA (installable, offline basics, push) | Rosnące | repeat-use services, staff tools |
| Privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible, Umami, Matomo) | Rosnące | less consent friction |
| Consent Mode v2, server-side tagging | Sprawdzone for ads | required for Google ads measurement in the EEA |

## 6. Trust and compliance
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility (EAA since 28 Jun 2025) | Obowiązkowe dla wielu sklepów | WCAG 2.2 AA, accessibility statement |
| Omnibus (lowest 30-day price, review verification) | Obowiązkowe | shops and anyone showing reviews |
| GPSR product data | Obowiązkowe | physical products in the EU |
| AI transparency (EU AI Act Art. 50) | Rosnące | disclose chatbots and AI-generated content; check current application dates, parts were proposed for delay |
| Real faces, company data, guarantees | Sprawdzone | trust for local and high-ticket purchases |

## 7. Content
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Case studies with real numbers | Sprawdzone | services, B2B, agencies |
| Price explainer pages ("ile kosztuje…") | Sprawdzone | top intent for services |
| Expert/author pages (E-E-A-T) | Sprawdzone | health, finance, legal, B2B |
| Short-form video embedded on pages | Rosnące | how-to, behind the scenes |
| AI-written mass content | Ryzyko | thin, generic, hurts trust and rankings |

## 8. Retention
| Trend | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lifecycle e-mail/SMS (welcome, abandoned cart, replenish, win-back, review request) | Sprawdzone | biggest revenue lever for shops |
| Loyalty and referral programs | Sprawdzone | repeat-purchase niches |
| Accounts with real value (tracking, reorder, invoices, bookings) | Sprawdzone | don't force accounts for first purchase |
| Push notifications via PWA | Rosnące | only opt-in, valuable messages |
