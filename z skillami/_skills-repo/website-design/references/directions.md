# Style directions

Starting points, not templates. Always adapt palette and details to the concept of the
business. Hex values are proposals; check contrast for every pair you use. Font names
are proposals; verify Polish support (Google Fonts → Language: Polish) and test
„Zażółć gęślą jaźń ŁÓDŹ” in each weight.

## 1. Editorial Calm
- **Mood:** quiet confidence, like a well-made magazine or apothecary.
- **Palette:** bg `#f4efe6`, text `#1e1c19`, muted `#5b554c`, accent `#8a5a3b`
  (terracotta-brown, ≈5:1 on bg), secondary `#5f6b4a` (olive).
- **Type:** headings Newsreader or Fraunces (400–600), text Inter or Source Sans 3;
  large serif headlines, small caps labels.
- **Layout:** generous white space, asymmetric editorial grid, large photos, narrow text
  columns, thin rules as dividers.
- **Motion:** slow, soft fades 400–600 ms; image reveal with slight scale 1.04 → 1.
- **References:** Aesop (restraint, warm palette), Kinfolk-style editorial layouts.
- **Use for:** cosmetics, wellness, boutique hotels, premium bakeries, interior studios.
- **Avoid for:** discount shops, urgent services.

## 2. Swiss Precision
- **Mood:** clear, rational, precise.
- **Palette:** bg `#ffffff`, text `#111111`, muted `#5e5e5e`, accent `#e2231a` (signal red)
  or `#1f4bd8` (blue), surface `#f2f2f2`.
- **Type:** Inter Tight, Instrument Sans or Archivo; big numbers, tight headings,
  tabular figures.
- **Layout:** strict 12-col grid, left-aligned, strong horizontal rules, data tables,
  large indexes (01, 02, 03).
- **Motion:** snappy 150–250 ms, line-draw and number counters (real numbers only).
- **References:** Vercel (grid precision), Swiss poster tradition, Vignelli Canon.
- **Use for:** B2B, architecture, engineering, consultancies, SaaS.
- **Avoid for:** cosy local food, children.

## 3. Industrial Trade
- **Mood:** strong, honest, hands-on — like a well-kept company van.
- **Palette:** bg `#f3efe8` (stone cream), dark `#23262a` (graphite), surface/border
  `#c9c2b6` (concrete), accent `#b4532a` (brick; white text on it ≈ 5:1; for small text on cream use darker `#9a4422`), success
  `#2f7d4f`.
- **Type:** headings Archivo (bold/expanded or condensed widths) or Barlow Condensed,
  text IBM Plex Sans; uppercase labels with letter-spacing.
- **Layout:** bold blocks, dark/light alternation, before/after as hero, price tables,
  big phone number, sticky mobile action bar.
- **Motion:** mechanical and quick: wipes, slider reveals, counters of real data.
- **References:** Bellroy (explainers) for clarity; workwear brand sites for tone.
- **Use for:** paving/cleaning, construction, renovation, automotive, logistics.
- **Avoid for:** luxury beauty, fine dining.

## 4. Warm Craft
- **Mood:** handmade, friendly, local.
- **Palette:** bg `#fbf5ea`, text `#2b1d14`, accent `#c9822b` (mustard — use as fill
  with dark text, not as text on cream), secondary `#7a3e2b` (roasted brown),
  surface `#f1e6d3`.
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft, 500–700) + Work Sans; hand-drawn accents only if custom
  and consistent.
- **Layout:** photo-led, slightly irregular grids, paper texture subtle (≤ 4% noise),
  stickers/labels inspired by real packaging.
- **Motion:** gentle bounce on hover (spring, small amplitude), product tilt.
- **References:** Graza (packaging-led playfulness), Our Place (colour and lifestyle).
- **Use for:** bakeries, cafés, coffee roasters, handmade, local food.
- **Avoid for:** corporate B2B, medical.

## 5. Premium Tech
- **Mood:** precise, premium, product-as-hero.
- **Palette:** light: bg `#f5f5f7`, text `#1d1d1f`, accent `#0071e3`; dark: bg `#0b0b0c`,
  text `#f5f5f7`, muted `#a1a1a6`.
- **Type:** Geist or Inter (tight tracking at display sizes) + mono for specs.
- **Layout:** centred product stages, huge type, sticky scroll sequences, spec grids.
- **Motion:** scroll-driven storytelling (GSAP ScrollTrigger / CSS scroll timelines),
  smooth 300–500 ms transitions, product rotation if 3D assets exist.
- **References:** Apple (scroll storytelling), Nothing (consistent system), Oura.
- **Use for:** electronics, apps, hardware startups, premium gadgets.
- **Avoid for:** local services without product imagery.

## 6. Bold Brand
- **Mood:** loud, confident, memorable.
- **Palette:** one saturated brand colour as large fields (e.g. `#ff5a1f` — ink text on it ≈ 5.6:1, white only ≈ 3:1), ink `#1a1a1a`,
  paper `#fff2e0`; strict rules where each colour appears.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque or Unbounded for display, DM Sans for text; oversized
  headlines, tight leading.
- **Layout:** colour blocks, big cut-out product photos, marquee strips used once.
- **Motion:** energetic but short: pop-in scale 0.95 → 1, marquee, hover shifts.
- **References:** Liquid Death (voice), Gymshark (drops energy).
- **Use for:** drinks, streetwear, events, youth brands — only with a strong brand.
- **Risk:** easily becomes AI slop; keep a tight system and real brand assets.

## 7. Luxury Minimal
- **Mood:** quiet luxury, space, precision.
- **Palette:** bg `#f2ede4` (ivory) or `#0e0e0e`, text `#111111` / `#f2ede4`, accent
  `#b08d57` (muted gold — on dark backgrounds; too weak as text on ivory).
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display (display only) + Manrope; wide
  letter-spacing for small uppercase labels.
- **Layout:** large photography, very few elements per screen, thin lines, centred
  compositions used deliberately.
- **Motion:** slow 600–900 ms fades and image crossfades; no bouncing.
- **References:** Aesop, high-end fashion houses (study restraint, not logos).
- **Use for:** jewellery, premium fashion, premium real estate, weddings, fine dining.

## 8. Nature / Organic
- **Mood:** fresh, grounded, outdoor.
- **Palette:** bg `#f1ede3` (sand), text `#1f2a1e`, primary `#3f5a3c` (moss; white text
  ≈ 7:1), secondary `#a9b8a0` (sage, decorative), accent `#c96f3b` (clay).
- **Type:** Literata or Source Serif 4 + DM Sans.
- **Layout:** real landscape/material photography, organic crops (from photos, not
  random blobs), seasonal content blocks.
- **Motion:** calm parallax on one image max, soft reveals.
- **References:** Patagonia (mission content), Allbirds (material storytelling).
- **Use for:** gardening, eco products, outdoor, agritourism, wellness.

## 9. Clinical Clear
- **Mood:** trustworthy, calm, clear.
- **Palette:** bg `#ffffff`, surface `#f2f7f7`, text `#14232b`, primary `#0f6e6e` (teal;
  white text ≈ 6:1), accent `#e9a23b` (warm highlight for CTAs with dark text).
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans or Onest; optionally Source Serif 4 for articles.
- **Layout:** clean cards for services, team photos with roles, booking always visible,
  FAQ prominent.
- **Motion:** minimal; feedback only.
- **References:** good patient-first clinic sites; NN/g for form clarity.
- **Use for:** clinics, dentists, physio, psychologists, education.

## 10. Dark Studio
- **Mood:** creative, cinematic.
- **Palette:** bg `#111214`, text `#ecebe8`, muted `#9b9a96`, one luminous accent
  (`#c6f432` lime or `#7aa2ff`) used sparingly.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk or Instrument Sans + Inter; huge display type.
- **Layout:** full-screen case studies, horizontal galleries, custom cursor (desktop
  only, never required for function).
- **Motion:** rich but purposeful: page transitions (View Transitions), image
  distortion on hover only if performance allows.
- **References:** Locomotive, darkroom.engineering, Awwwards SOTD portfolios.
- **Use for:** agencies, photographers, musicians, game studios.
- **Avoid for:** local services, clinics, anything where trust and clarity sell.

## 11. Retro-Modern
- **Mood:** nostalgic character with modern usability.
- **Palette:** bg `#f1e4cc`, ink `#2e2a26`, orange `#d9541e` (large type and fills only, ≈ 3.2:1 on bg), green `#2f5d50`.
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft/wonky axes) or a period-inspired display face with Polish
  support + Rubik.
- **Layout:** badges and frames inspired by the era, but modern grid and spacing.
- **Motion:** playful but quick; no gimmicks that hurt reading.
- **Use for:** barbers, vintage shops, coffee, craft beer, diners.

## 12. Choosing and mixing
- Choose from the business concept and audience, not from personal taste.
- Mix carefully: one base direction + one borrowed element (e.g. Industrial Trade with
  Editorial Calm's typography for a premium renovation firm).
- Present 3 directions that are really different; recommend one with a reason tied to
  the customer ("klienci to właściciele domów 35–60 lat — liczy się zaufanie
  i konkret, dlatego Industrial Trade").
