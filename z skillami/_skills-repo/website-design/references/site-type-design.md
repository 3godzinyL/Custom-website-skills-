# Design by site type

What the design must emphasize for each kind of site. Use it to choose directions for
the demos and to judge them. "Motion" = how much animation fits (low / medium / high).

| Type | Design must emphasize | Hero pattern | Key components | Imagery | Type & colour tendency | Motion | Mobile specifics | Pitfalls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Local services** (cleaning, paving, renovation, plumbing) | trust, concreteness, fast contact | headline with promise + area + before/after or real crew photo, 2 CTAs (call, quote) | price-from cards, before/after slider, process steps, Google rating, area map, quote form | real jobs, consistent angles, no stock | strong sans or condensed headings, sturdy palette (graphite, stone, one work-colour accent) | low–medium (slider, reveals) | sticky call/quote bar, click-to-call, short form | stock photos, no prices, decorative fluff, tiny phone number |
| **Beauty, barber, wellness** | atmosphere + easy booking | mood photo or short loop + "Zarezerwuj" | service menu with duration/price, team cards, booking widget, gallery, vouchers | own interiors and results (consent) | elegant serif or refined sans, soft or rich palette from the salon | medium (soft fades) | booking always one tap away | booking hidden, PDF price list, generic spa stock |
| **Health / clinic / physio** | calm, clarity, credibility | clear headline + specialists + booking | doctor cards with credentials, services, prices, preparation info, FAQ, location | real staff and place, no clichés of stethoscopes | clean sans, calm blues/greens/teals, high contrast | low | booking + phone, large text | cold clinical sterility, low contrast pastels |
| **Restaurant / café / bakery** | appetite, place, practical info | full-bleed food/interior photo + hours + reserve/order | HTML menu with prices and allergens, hours, map, reservation, specials, events | professional food photography, warm light | characterful serif or display + readable sans, warm palette | medium (image reveals) | menu and hours first, call, map | menu as PDF, outdated hours, dark unreadable photos |
| **Fashion e-commerce** | product and model imagery, fast browsing | campaign image or new drop + shop CTA | product grid, filters, size guide, product gallery, lookbooks | consistent studio + on-model + detail shots | brand-led; often minimal to let photos lead | medium (hover image swap, quick view) | thumb-friendly filters, swipe galleries, sticky add-to-cart | slow images, missing size info, cluttered grids |
| **Cosmetics / food / consumables shop** | ingredients, ritual, subscription value | hero product + benefit + shop | product benefits, ingredients, routine builder/quiz, reviews with photos, subscriptions | product + texture + lifestyle | soft editorial or bold brand, colour from packaging | medium | quick add, subscription toggle | fake claims, unreadable ingredient lists |
| **Electronics / furniture / high-consideration** | specs clarity, product storytelling, confidence | product hero + key spec numbers | spec tables, comparison, 3D/AR or 360° views, delivery/warranty info, financing | renders + real context photos | precise sans, neutral palette, one accent | medium–high (scroll storytelling) | spec accordions, compare on mobile | spec walls without hierarchy, heavy 3D that kills performance |
| **Small brand / handmade** | maker, uniqueness, warmth | maker/workshop photo + product | story, process, product cards, custom order form, gift options | behind-the-scenes, hands, materials | warm craft serif + sans, natural palette | low–medium | story short, shop fast | over-cute illustration, cluttered pages |
| **SaaS / app** | the product itself, outcomes | outcome headline + real product UI | feature sections with real screenshots, pricing table, logos (real), integrations, FAQ, changelog | real UI, short demos/video | precise sans, product-matching palette, dark or light deliberately | medium (UI demos) | simplified screenshots, readable pricing | abstract gradients, fake dashboards, "platform for growth" |
| **Agency / portfolio / creative** | the work, taste, personality | statement + selected work | case study layouts, work grid, process, team, contact brief | large project imagery, video | expressive display type, bold or minimal | high (transitions, cursor effects on desktop only) | work first, fast loading | style over content, unreadable experimental nav |
| **Personal brand / coach / courses** | the person, results, offer clarity | portrait + promise + CTA | offer cards, testimonials (real), lead magnet, programme outline, FAQ | authentic portraits, real events | friendly sans/serif mix, personal colour | low–medium | lead magnet and booking easy | guru clichés, walls of text |
| **Real estate / developer** | location, units, clarity of choice | building/area render + "Zobacz mieszkania" | unit finder with filters, floor plans, availability table, map, gallery, mortgage calc | renders + real area photos | calm premium sans/serif, neutral palette | medium (building selector) | filters and plans readable, call/viewing | slow renders, unclear availability |
| **Hotel / tourism** | experience, rooms, direct booking | immersive photo/video + booking bar | booking widget, room cards, amenities, location, reviews, packages | professional, seasonal, real | elegant, place-inspired palette | medium | booking bar sticky, dates easy | booking only via OTA link, slow galleries |
| **Fitness / sports / dance** | energy, schedule, sign-up | action photo/video + trial CTA | schedule table/calendar, memberships, trainers, facilities, sign-up | real classes and people | bold condensed type, energetic palette | medium–high | schedule usable on phone | unreadable schedules, stock athletes |
| **B2B / industrial** | competence, specs, RFQ | capability statement + industries + RFQ CTA | product/capability catalogue, datasheets, certifications, case studies, RFQ form | real plant, products, team | Swiss/industrial precision, restrained palette | low | catalogue search, downloads | brochure-like "about us" walls, hidden products |
| **NGO / foundation** | mission, impact, donating | story photo + impact + donate | impact numbers (real), stories, donate widget, transparency, volunteering | real people (consent), dignity | warm, human, accessible palette | low–medium | donate in one tap | guilt-trip imagery, hidden donate |
| **Blog / media** | reading comfort, discovery | latest/featured story | article template, categories, author, related, newsletter | editorial images | readable serif or sans, generous measure | low | reading-first, fast | ads breaking layout, tiny text |

## Preparation checklist before designing any type

- [ ] Main action and secondary action known.
- [ ] Real photos available? If not: photo shot list for the owner + layouts that work
      with placeholders.
- [ ] Logo as SVG; brand colours/fonts to keep identified.
- [ ] Real content lengths (longest service name, longest price line, longest review).
- [ ] Competitor look known (to stand out, not copy).
- [ ] Audience age and devices (older audiences → larger text, higher contrast).
