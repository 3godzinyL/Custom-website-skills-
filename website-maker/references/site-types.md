# Site types: must-haves and trending extras

For each type: **Goal/CTA**, **Must-have** (missing = lost customers), **Trending /
bestseller extras** (candidates for "Propozycje spoza Twojej listy"), **Schema.org**.
Built-in knowledge as of 2026 — confirm with live research when tools allow.
If a site mixes types (e.g. bakery + shop), combine the lists.

## 1. Local services
- **Goal:** call / quote request / booking a visit.
- **Must-have:** service area (map + list of towns), services with price ranges
  ("od X zł/m²"), before/after gallery of real jobs, process steps, guarantee, equipment
  and materials used, real reviews (Google), click-to-call sticky bar on mobile, quote
  form with photo upload, availability/response time, company data, FAQ (price,
  duration, what the customer must prepare, weather).
- **Trending extras:** instant price calculator (area × service × options), interactive
  before/after slider, online booking of a visit date, WhatsApp/SMS contact, seasonal
  packages and subscriptions (e.g. yearly maintenance), Google review request after
  job, short video of real work, case-study pages per town (local SEO), vouchers.
- **Schema:** LocalBusiness (specific subtype), Service, FAQPage, Review (only real).

## 2. Appointments: beauty, barber, physio, clinic, dentist
- **Goal:** book an appointment.
- **Must-have:** online booking with real availability (Booksy/Versum/own system or
  integration), price list per service with duration, team with photos and
  specialisations, location with parking/transport, opening hours, cancellation policy,
  reviews, gallery of results (with consent), contact.
- **Trending extras:** pick-your-specialist booking, deposit/prepayment, reminders
  SMS/e-mail, gift vouchers bought online, loyalty program, packages, before/after
  per treatment, treatment guides (SEO), waitlist for cancelled slots, for medical:
  NFZ/private info, preparation instructions, patient portal.
- **Schema:** BeautySalon / HairSalon / MedicalClinic / Dentist / Physician.

## 3. Restaurant, café, bakery, catering
- **Goal:** visit, reserve a table, order.
- **Must-have:** menu as HTML (not only PDF) with prices and allergens, opening hours
  (incl. holidays), address + map + parking, reservation, phone, real food and interior
  photos, delivery/ordering options, events/private dining info.
- **Trending extras:** own online ordering (avoid aggregator fees) with pickup slots,
  daily specials/lunch menu updated from the backend, pre-orders for bakery (cakes,
  holidays), gift cards, QR menu, newsletter/SMS for specials, catering configurator,
  Instagram feed, story of the place/chef, loyalty stamps.
- **Schema:** Restaurant / CafeOrCoffeeShop / Bakery, Menu, OpeningHoursSpecification.

## 4. Online shop — general rules
- **Goal:** purchase.
- **Must-have:** clear categories + search, filters and sorting, product page with
  multiple photos/zoom, variants, price with VAT, availability, delivery time and cost
  before checkout, returns info, reviews, size/spec info, cart with editable quantities,
  guest checkout, Polish payment methods (BLIK, fast transfers via Przelewy24/PayU/Tpay,
  cards, Apple/Google Pay), InPost Paczkomaty + courier, order confirmation and status
  e-mails, account (optional), regulamin, returns form, Omnibus lowest-price info,
  GPSR product data, invoice with NIP, trust badges only if real.
- **Trending extras:** free-shipping threshold progress bar, buy now pay later (PayPo,
  Twisto, Klarna-type), product bundles and "frequently bought together", recently
  viewed, wishlist, back-in-stock alerts, abandoned cart e-mails, reviews with photos,
  quick view, predictive search, product comparison, loyalty points, referral codes,
  express checkout, Google Merchant feed, Allegro integration, post-purchase upsell,
  UGC gallery, live chat/AI shopping assistant.
- **Schema:** Product, Offer, AggregateRating (real), BreadcrumbList, Organization.

## 5. Shop: fashion and apparel
- **Must-have:** size charts per product, fit info ("model ma 178 cm, nosi M"),
  colour swatches, many photos incl. on-model and detail, material and care, free
  returns info, filters (size, colour, price, fit).
- **Trending extras:** size recommender/fit quiz, shoppable lookbooks, "complete the
  look", short video on product page, drops/limited editions with countdown and
  notify-me, resale/repair program, sustainability info, try-before-you-buy.

## 6. Shop: cosmetics, food, supplements, consumables
- **Must-have:** ingredients/INCI or composition, nutrition facts, usage, warnings,
  certificates (only real), expiry/storage, delivery temperature rules for food.
- **Trending extras:** subscriptions ("subskrybuj i oszczędzaj"), routine/regimen
  builder or quiz, refills, bundles and sample packs, reviews filtered by skin type,
  recipes/usage content, loyalty program.

## 7. Shop: electronics, furniture, high-consideration products
- **Must-have:** detailed specs table, comparison, dimensions, compatibility, warranty,
  installation/delivery service info, manuals/downloads, Q&A.
- **Trending extras:** 3D/AR viewer ("zobacz w swoim pokoju"), configurator with live
  price, financing/instalments, showroom booking, video demos, trade-in, extended
  warranty, sample ordering (fabrics/colours), room inspiration galleries.

## 8. Small brand / handmade / art shop
- **Must-have:** maker story with real photos, process, uniqueness info (each piece
  differs), lead times, custom order form, care instructions.
- **Trending extras:** limited drops + waitlist, personalisation (engraving, names),
  behind-the-scenes content, newsletter-first launches, gift wrapping and notes,
  community/UGC gallery, workshops/events booking.

## 9. SaaS, app, startup
- **Goal:** sign up / start trial / book demo.
- **Must-have:** value proposition above the fold, product visuals (real UI), features
  by outcome, pricing page with plan comparison, social proof (logos, case studies —
  real), security/privacy page, docs/help, changelog, sign-up/login, FAQ, contact sales.
- **Trending extras:** interactive product tour/demo, ROI calculator, templates gallery,
  integrations directory, comparison pages vs competitors, status page, community,
  free tools (lead magnets), AI-powered onboarding, usage-based pricing calculator.
- **Schema:** SoftwareApplication, Organization, FAQPage.

## 10. Agency, freelancer, portfolio, studio
- **Goal:** inquiry / call booking.
- **Must-have:** selected work with case studies (problem → process → result with real
  numbers), services, process, team/person, clients (real), testimonials, clear contact
  with project brief form (budget ranges, timeline).
- **Trending extras:** calendar booking (Cal.com/Calendly), filterable work grid,
  case-study storytelling with motion, pricing packages/starting prices, insights/blog,
  awards (real), availability indicator ("wolne terminy od marca").
- **Schema:** Organization / ProfessionalService / Person, CreativeWork.

## 11. Personal brand, coach, online courses
- **Must-have:** who you are + credibility, offer (1:1, group, course) with prices,
  results/testimonials (real), lead magnet + newsletter, booking/checkout, FAQ, content.
- **Trending extras:** course platform with progress, community area, webinar
  registration with replays, quizzes/self-assessment, payment plans, affiliate
  program, podcast/YouTube integration, digital product shop.

## 12. Real estate, developer
- **Must-have:** listings/units with filters (price, area, rooms, floor), floor plans,
  availability table, location map with surroundings, gallery/renders, standard of
  finishing, prices (in PL developers must publish prices — check current rules),
  contact/viewing booking, investment schedule.
- **Trending extras:** interactive building/floor selector, 3D virtual tours, mortgage
  calculator, price history, unit comparison, favourites, progress camera/updates.

## 13. Hotel, apartments, tourism
- **Must-have:** booking engine with live availability and prices (direct booking),
  room types with photos and amenities, location and attractions, policies (check-in,
  cancellation, pets), reviews, contact, multi-language.
- **Trending extras:** best-price guarantee for direct booking, packages (spa,
  romantic), vouchers, local guide content, upsells (breakfast, late checkout),
  virtual tours, chat, loyalty for returning guests.
- **Schema:** Hotel / LodgingBusiness / Accommodation.

## 14. Events, weddings, conferences
- **Must-have:** date, place, agenda/programme, speakers/performers, tickets with
  pricing tiers, venue info and access, FAQ, sponsors, contact.
- **Trending extras:** countdown, add-to-calendar, ticket QR + check-in, networking
  app, live stream/replays, early-bird and group tickets, waitlist.
- **Schema:** Event.

## 15. Fitness, gym, sports club, dance school
- **Must-have:** class schedule (live from backend), memberships and prices, trainers,
  facilities gallery, location/hours, sign-up/trial, Multisport/Medicover-type card
  acceptance info (PL), rules.
- **Trending extras:** class booking with limits and waitlist, member app/portal,
  free trial flow, transformation stories (real, with consent), online classes,
  challenges, referral program.
- **Schema:** SportsActivityLocation / ExerciseGym.

## 16. Automotive (workshop, detailing, dealer)
- **Must-have:** services with prices from, booking a slot, brands served, location,
  hours, before/after (detailing), warranty, reviews; dealers: inventory with filters,
  vehicle pages with VIN/specs/photos, financing.
- **Trending extras:** online diagnosis request with photos, service reminders,
  live repair status, packages (ceramic coating tiers), trade-in valuation form.

## 17. B2B, manufacturer, industrial
- **Must-have:** product/capability catalogue with specs, industries served,
  certifications (real), downloadable datasheets, references/case studies, RFQ
  (request for quote) form with file upload, contact per department, company facts.
- **Trending extras:** B2B portal with prices per client, product configurator,
  CAD/BIM downloads, distributor locator, multi-language, sample requests, sustainability
  reporting, ERP integration.

## 18. Education, school, kindergarten, tutoring
- **Must-have:** offer/programme, staff, schedule, fees, enrolment process and dates,
  location, gallery, parent info, contact, documents (statutes, rules).
- **Trending extras:** online enrolment with document upload, parent portal/news,
  events calendar, virtual tour, free trial lesson booking, newsletter.

## 19. NGO, foundation
- **Must-have:** mission, concrete projects and results, transparency (KRS, reports),
  donate button with PL payment methods, 1,5% podatku info (PL), volunteering, contact.
- **Trending extras:** recurring donations, fundraising goal progress, impact
  calculator ("50 zł = …"), stories, campaign landing pages, donor wall (with consent).

## 20. Blog, media, content site
- **Must-have:** fast article template, readable typography, categories/tags, search,
  author pages, related posts, newsletter, comments or discussion (moderated), ads/
  monetisation slots that don't wreck layout, RSS.
- **Trending extras:** paywall/membership, podcasts/video embeds, table of contents,
  reading progress, bookmarks, AI search over archive, topic hubs (SEO).
- **Schema:** Article / BlogPosting, Person, BreadcrumbList.
